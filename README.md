## About me

I have **14 years in software engineering and R&D**, including **6+ years of C++ practice** and **5 years in commercial C++ roles**. My recent focus is embedded telephony: C++17/Qt software on ARM64 Linux devices, SIP/VoIP behavior, and the tooling needed to configure and maintain real products.

Before that, I worked on long-lived C89 production systems, industrial C++ software, and **7+ years of satellite and UAV image-processing R&D** in MATLAB.

I particularly enjoy debugging problems that cross boundaries: a UI symptom might originate in a SIP state machine, device configuration, a Linux service, or the hardware itself.
